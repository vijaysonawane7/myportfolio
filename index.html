01 — About

Engineer first. Problem‑solver always.

I build apps — I take a product idea and carry it all the way to something people actually open and use. I’m a software engineer who cares about the unglamorous things that make products great: correctness, latency, error budgets, and code the next person can read at 2 a.m. without cursing my name.

Most of my time goes into designing backends and APIs that stay calm under load, then wiring them to front‑ends that feel instant. I like shipping small, measuring everything, and deleting more code than I add.

Alongside engineering, I run Sona Enterprises — my own company, where I take projects from first conversation to production: scoping the problem, designing the system, writing the code, and standing behind it after launch.

When I’m not writing code, I’m reading post‑mortems, breaking my own side projects on purpose, and learning whatever the thing I got wrong last quarter was trying to teach me.
